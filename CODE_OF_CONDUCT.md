# Code of Conduct

Optune is a friendly project. Everyone who takes part — in issues, pull requests, discussions and any other project space — is expected to:

- be respectful and assume good faith,
- give and accept feedback kindly and keep it about the work,
- welcome newcomers and people with different backgrounds and experience,
- respect others' privacy: no sharing of private information without consent.

Not acceptable: harassment, discrimination, personal attacks, trolling, sexualised language or attention, threats, doxxing, or spam.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit, hide, lock or remove comments, issues and contributions, and may block people who don't follow this code. To report a problem, open an issue (or use a [private advisory](https://github.com/Sanjays2402/optune/security/advisories/new) for anything sensitive). Reports are handled confidentially where possible.

This code applies in project spaces and when someone is representing the project in public.
